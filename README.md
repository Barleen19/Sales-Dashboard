# Sales-Dashboard
Introduction : This project presents an interactive Sales Dashboard developed in Power BI to visualize and analyze a company’s overall sales performance. The dashboard consolidates sales, profit, and customer data from multiple regions and product categories, offering a clear and data-driven view of business performance.

Business Problem : The company faced challenges in tracking real-time sales trends and understanding which regions, categories, and segments contributed most to revenue and profit. Manual data analysis was time-consuming and prone to inaccuracies, making it difficult for decision-makers to identify growth opportunities and improve operational efficiency.

Dashboard Visuals :
1. The Power BI dashboard includes multiple interactive visuals designed for comprehensive analysis:
2. KPI Cards showing total sales, profit, quantity sold, and average processing days.
3. Bar Charts for Sales by Category, Sub-Category, and Ship Mode to identify top-performing areas.
4. Map Visualization displaying Sales and Profit by State for geographical insights.
5. Line and Area Charts showcasing Monthly Sales and Profit Trends over time.
6. Donut Chart representing Sales by Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).
7. Regional Filters (Central, East, West, South) for focused regional performance analysis.

Key Insights :
1. Highest Sales Region: The West region, with California leading at over 335K in total sales.
2. Top Product Categories: Office Supplies and Technology generated the highest revenue.
3. Shipping Performance: Standard Class mode contributed the majority of sales volume.
4. Customer Segments: Consumer Segment accounted for nearly 48% of total sales.
5. Profit Trend: Profit increased steadily towards year-end, indicating strong seasonal performance.

Tools Used :
1. Microsoft Power BI – For building interactive dashboards and reports.
2. Microsoft Excel / CSV – For data cleaning, preparation, and integration.

Objective : To deliver a dynamic, insight-driven Power BI dashboard that empowers data-driven decisions by transforming raw sales data into clear, actionable business intelligence.
